# Room 04 — current source story

From the road, the house has one floor.

A blue chair stands outside. There is a white scratch across its back, roughly the length of a fingernail. You rest your suitcase against it, with the luggage tag facing the wall.

The front door opens until it touches another chair.

You could probably squeeze through.

Around the side, the grass has been walked flat.

The back door has been repainted. Its number shows through when the light catches it.

**04.**

Inside, a wall fan turns towards a desk, clicks, and turns away. Someone has tied a bottle cap to the end of its pull cord.

You move a chair out of the passage. One foot catches between the tiles. You push again.

A cup rattles.

The door swings shut.

For a while, you rinse the cup under a tap that takes several turns to stop dripping.

Through the window beside the sink, there is a swimming pool one floor below. A towel hangs over a railing. You take the corridor on the left.

Without coming to any stairs, you reach the water.

It is warm around your shoes.

The blue chair is beside the pool. A folded towel rests on its seat, leaving the white scratch visible.

Along the windowsill, several flowerpots have been placed on different-sized plates. One plate is decorated with a blue fish. You saw another piece of that pattern beside the sink, though you cannot remember whether it was a cup or a bowl.

The next room has a table.

You bring your cup with you.

There are chairs around three sides. Against the fourth, someone has set a narrow bench with a cushion folded over one end.

A photograph is tucked beneath the bench’s short leg.

You lift it carefully.

Most of the picture is covered by a sleeve. What remains shows part of a table, two cups, and a hand at the edge of the frame. It might be passing something. The rest has been cut off.

On the back, in ordinary blue pen:

*I put it back before I left.*

There is no name.

You find a thin piece of cardboard on the windowsill and fold it beneath the short leg. It takes two attempts to stop the bench rocking.

Then you sit with the photograph beside your cup.

Outside the window, someone has left a pair of shoes to dry.

They are facing the house.
